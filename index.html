NuMind

Knowledge worth keeping.

NuMind is a project to organize useful human knowledge into lessons, manuals, and references people can read, keep, and pass on.

  • Learn

    Organize material by subject and skill level so someone can find a starting point and build on it.

  • Apply

    Develop practical guides for subjects such as science, engineering, agriculture, history, and construction.

  • Preserve

    Plan for cited, multilingual resources in digital, offline, and physical formats.
