NuMind

Knowledge worth keeping.

NuMind aims to turn reliable source material into lessons, manuals, and references people can learn from and pass on.

See the priorities

Priorities

  • Organize

    Arrange material by subject and skill level so readers can find a starting point.

  • Make it practical

    Develop guides for real tasks, from understanding basic science to growing food and building things.

  • Keep it accessible

    Design for print, offline use, and translation so resources can reach more readers.

Where it stands

NuMind is defining how to organize, cite, and preserve its materials. A public library is a future milestone.
