Priorities

    Design for print, offline use, and translation so resources can reach more readers.

Featured project

Guide review checklist

Starter available. Turn a reliable source into a short guide someone else can use. The first planned trial will use a low-risk task, such as labeling and inventorying a shared bookshelf.

  1. Record the source, its edition, and the conditions the guide applies to.
  2. Save or print a copy and ask another reader to follow it offline.
  3. Record what the reader finished, where they got stuck, and what to change.

What informs the work

OpenStax has subject specialists review textbooks before publication. NuMind will record the reviewer’s role, the review date, and what changed. OpenStax’s review process

Kiwix readers download collections ahead of time. To check whether a guide is useful offline, ask a reader to try it from a saved copy and note which steps still need help. Kiwix Reader

The VATRA library

NuMind plans to catalog books worth passing on, from literature, philosophy, and science to practical manuals for growing food, building, and repair. The goal is a physical library for VATRA and offline digital copies of editions that can be shared.

Common questions

What can I use today?
Start with the guide review checklist or browse the reading resources. Each linked resource is maintained by its own project.
Can I use the materials offline?
The checklist is a plain-text download you can save or print. The offline libraries link to projects that explain how to download their collections.
Will the VATRA library include digital copies?
The plan is to catalog each edition and share digital copies where the rights allow it. See the library plan.
